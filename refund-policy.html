Refund Policy

At Island Barter Exchange, we strive to provide our members with quality services and a seamless experience. Our services are based on a prepayment system (the cash balance account) where members prepay into their accounts, from which fees or commissions are drawn. Due to the nature of our business model, we generally do not offer refunds.

Please read the following details carefully.

1. Cash Balance Accounts

Members of Island Barter Exchange are required to prepay into their account, and fees for transactions and other applicable charges are deducted from this prepaid balance. By prepaying into your account, you acknowledge and agree that the funds are non-refundable.
